01 Research software, not a medical device

Tracking Cube, the Portal, iRNDA training, and the RNDA manuals and scoring forms published here are research software and research materials, built and run by a research group to support approved research.

They are not supplied as a therapeutic good or medical device and are not entered on the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods. Use is limited to the purposes, sites, and people covered by the approved ethics application; use outside that scope is not authorised.

02 Not a diagnosis or clinical advice

Scores, summaries, charts, and reports produced by these apps are aids to assessment. They are not a diagnosis and not a clinical recommendation.

Output must be interpreted by a suitably qualified clinician, within their scope of practice, alongside clinical judgement and other assessment information. Nothing here creates a clinician–patient relationship or replaces professional advice, and decisions about a person’s care remain with the treating clinician and their health service.

Not for emergencies. These apps are not monitored for urgent clinical issues. If someone needs urgent care, call 000 or contact the treating health service directly.
