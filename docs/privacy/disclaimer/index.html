Important information about using the Tracking Cube

This information is provided to help you understand what the Tracking Cube and its associated resources are designed to do, and the limits of their use. It is general information and is not legal advice.

For research involving participants, the approved ethics documents and Participant Information and Consent Form for that research project apply.

01 The Tracking Cube is used for research

The Tracking Cube, its Portal, the iRNDA training, and the RNDA manuals and scoring forms are developed and managed by a research group to support approved research.

These resources are research tools and materials. They are not supplied as therapeutic goods or medical devices and are not included on the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods.

Access is provided for particular research projects, sites and authorised users. Please only use the Tracking Cube and its resources for the purposes for which you have been given access.

02 The Tracking Cube does not make a diagnosis

The Tracking Cube may produce scores, summaries, charts and reports to help practitioners gather, organise and understand assessment information.

These outputs do not provide a diagnosis. They need to be considered by an appropriately qualified practitioner, within their scope of practice, together with their professional judgement and other relevant information about the child or young person.

Decisions about assessment, diagnosis, treatment or care remain the responsibility of the relevant practitioner and health service.

If someone needs urgent help

The Tracking Cube is not an emergency or crisis service, and information entered into the system is not monitored for urgent clinical concerns.

If someone is in immediate danger or needs urgent medical attention, call 000 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

For other urgent concerns, contact the person’s treating health service or an appropriate local service.

For 24-hour crisis support, access:

  • Lifeline: 13 11 14
  • Kids Helpline: 1800 55 1800
  • 13YARN: 13 92 76 for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people

03 Using the apps and materials

We work to keep the Tracking Cube and its resources available and up to date, but there may be times when the system is unavailable because of maintenance, updates or unexpected outages.

Manuals, scoring forms, training resources and other materials may also be updated or replaced as the research develops. Please make sure you are using the current version.

Tracking Cube materials are provided to authorised users for the purpose for which access was granted. Unless you have written permission, please do not redistribute, modify, translate or use these materials commercially.

The Tracking Cube™ and its logos are trademarks. The manuals, forms, training resources and website content are protected by copyright. If you would like permission to reuse or adapt any of these materials, please contact the Tracking Cube team through our support page.

There may be links to other websites or resources within the Tracking Cube that we think are useful. These websites are managed by other organisations, so we are not responsible for their content, availability or privacy practices.
