01 Notice text

TO CONFIRM: institution name collects your name, email address, organisation, and role to create your TrackingCube Ecosystem account and give you access to the apps you need.

There is no public sign-up — we created this account because you asked us for access. Your password is stored hashed by our identity provider, never in plaintext, and we keep login and security logs to operate and protect the service. All of this information is stored in Australia and is not disclosed overseas. If you do not finish activation we cannot create a working login.

This is not the participant information sheet — research data about participants is handled under the approved ethics application and PICF.

To access or correct your account details, or to make a privacy complaint, see the institution’s Privacy Policy TO CONFIRM: URL and the TrackingCube privacy statement.

02 How to show it

  • Put the notice above the password fields, not behind a footer link
  • Link the institution’s Privacy Policy and the privacy statement from the notice itself
  • Keep participant / PICF collection off this screen entirely
