01 What this page covers

The TrackingCube Ecosystem includes the Portal (portal.trackingcube.au), Tracking Cube (app.trackingcube.au, admin.trackingcube.au), and iRNDA training (irnda.trackingcube.au).

We are a research group at TO CONFIRM: institution name. The institution’s Privacy Policy is the governing document: TO CONFIRM: Privacy Policy URL. This page only adds the detail specific to these apps.

There are two kinds of information, handled differently:

  • Account information — about researchers and other authorised users. Explained on this page and in the collection notice shown at activation.
  • Participant information — about people taking part in research. Covered by the approved ethics protocol, the PICF, and the data management plan. It is not duplicated here, and it is never collected through the staff activation screen.

02 What we collect for your account

There is no public sign-up. You ask us for access and we create the account for you.

  • Name and email address
  • Organisation and the application roles set on your account
  • Sign-in data held by our identity provider — passwords are stored hashed, never in plaintext
  • Login and security logs, such as IP address, timestamps, and audit events

We use this to create and run your account, give you access to the apps you need, keep the service secure, and send activation and password-reset messages. We do not sell personal information and we do not use advertising cookies.

03 Where it is stored and who can see it

Everything the apps hold — accounts, sign-in data, application data, files, and logs — is stored and processed in Australia. We do not disclose personal information to overseas recipients. If that ever changes, this page and the data management plan will be updated first.

Account information is seen by authorised members of the research team, according to the roles assigned in the app, and by the institution’s privacy or IT security staff if there is an incident or a complaint. Otherwise we disclose it only where the law requires it.

We use HTTPS, role-based access, audit logs, and a secret manager for credentials. Accounts are disabled when someone leaves the team. Account records are kept only as long as we need them to run and secure the service; participant data is kept, destroyed, or de-identified as the approved protocol requires.

04 Questions, corrections and complaints

  • Your account details — ask us to correct them using the contact below.
  • Participant information — handled through the PICF and the HREC process, not through this page.
  • Complaints and data breaches — go to the institution’s privacy office, which follows the institution’s complaints and Notifiable Data Breaches process. You may also contact the OAIC at oaic.gov.au.
