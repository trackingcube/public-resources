Privacy and your Tracking Cube account

This notice explains how we handle personal information about people who have an account for the Tracking Cube systems.

The Tracking Cube systems include:

  • the Portal (portal.trackingcube.au)
  • Tracking Cube (app.trackingcube.au and admin.trackingcube.au)
  • iRNDA training (irnda.trackingcube.au)

The Tracking Cube research group is based at Griffith University. Griffith University’s Privacy Statement applies to the collection and handling of personal information. This notice provides some additional information about how personal information is handled within the Tracking Cube systems.

01 What information does this notice cover?

There are two main types of information within the Tracking Cube systems.

Your account information is information about you as an authorised user of the system. This includes information needed to create your account, give you the right level of access and keep the system secure.

Research participant information is information collected as part of an approved research project. This information is handled according to the relevant research ethics approval, Participant Information and Consent Form (PICF) and data management plan. Your account information is not part of research participant information.

02 What information do we collect about you?

There is no public sign-up for a Tracking Cube account. An account is created for you after your access has been approved by the Griffith team.

For your account, we will collect:

  • your name and email address
  • your organisation
  • the Tracking Cube applications and functions you are authorised to access
  • information needed to manage your sign-in securely
  • security and activity information, such as your IP address, login times and audit records.

We use this information to create and manage your account, provide access to the parts of Tracking Cube you are authorised to use, keep the system secure, and send messages such as account activation and password reset emails.

Your password is not stored as readable text.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

03 Where is information stored and who can access it?

All information held by the Tracking Cube apps, including account information, sign-in data, application data, files and security logs, is stored and processed in Australia. We do not send or disclose personal information to recipients outside Australia.

If this changes in the future, we will update this privacy notice and the relevant data management arrangements before any information is stored, processed or disclosed overseas.

Access to information within the Tracking Cube is limited according to each person’s role and the access they have been authorised to have. Authorised system administrators may access information when it is necessary to operate, maintain or secure the system. Authorised Griffith University privacy, information technology or cybersecurity staff may also access information where required to investigate or manage an incident or complaint.

Tracking Cube uses security measures including encrypted connections, role-based access controls and audit logging.

Accounts are disabled when a person no longer requires access to the system.

Account information is retained in accordance with Griffith University’s information and records management requirements. https://www.griffith.edu.au/information-management

Information collected about research participants is retained, de-identified or destroyed according to the requirements of the relevant research project and its approved data management arrangements. https://www.griffith.edu.au/research/research-services/ethics%2C-integrity-and-governance

04 Access, corrections, questions and complaints

You can ask to access or correct personal information Griffith University holds about you.

If your question relates to information collected about a research participant, please refer to the Participant Information and Consent Form for the relevant research project or contact the research team named in that form.

If you have a question about your Tracking Cube account, please contact:

  • Research group: yourtrack.org / contact email

Privacy questions or complaints can also be made directly to Griffith University’s Privacy Officer. Griffith University’s Privacy Statement explains how the University handles personal information and how you can make a privacy complaint https://www.griffith.edu.au/about-griffith/corporate-governance/right-to-information.

If you are not satisfied with Griffith University’s response to a privacy complaint, or the University has not responded within the required timeframe, you may be able to refer the complaint to the Queensland Office of the Information Commissioner.
