04 Account information we collect

There is no public self-registration. Access is requested, an administrator approves it, then we create an identity.

For accounts we typically collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Organisation / research group and application roles
  • Authentication data held by Auth0 (passwords are hashed by Auth0; we do not store plaintext passwords)
  • Email verification, last login, and similar login metadata
  • Login and security logs (for example IP address, timestamps, audit events)

If you do not complete activation we cannot give you a working login.

We use this information to create and manage your account, give you access to the apps you were approved for, keep the service secure, send activation and password-reset messages, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising cookies to profile you across other sites.

06 Overseas disclosure

Application and research data are hosted on AWS in Sydney, Australia.

Identity data is processed by Auth0. Auth0 may store or access that information outside Australia TO CONFIRM: Auth0 tenant region. This must match what was declared in the ethics data management plan.

07 Security and retention

We use HTTPS, encryption at rest where configured, a secret manager for credentials, role-based access, and audit logs. Accounts are approved before they can be used.

  • Participant data — kept, destroyed, or de-identified as the approved protocol / DMP requires (ethics-covered).
  • Staff accounts — disabled when someone leaves the team; remaining account records kept only as long as needed to run and secure the service TO CONFIRM: any institution retention rule.

08 Access, correction, and complaints

  • Your account — ask an administrator to correct details, or contact the address in section 9.
  • Participant information — the PICF and HREC process, not this page.
  • Complaints — institution privacy office first (section 9). You may also contact the OAIC: https://www.oaic.gov.au.
  • Data breach — institution Notifiable Data Breaches process; notify HREC if the approval requires it.
