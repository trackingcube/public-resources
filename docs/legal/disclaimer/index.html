01 Research software, not a medical device

TrackingCube and the wider YourTrack ecosystem — including Tracking Cube, the Portal, iRNDA training, and the RNDA manuals and scoring forms published here — are research software and research materials developed and operated by a research group.

They are provided to support approved research and the clinical-research workflows described in that approval. They are not supplied as a therapeutic good or medical device, and are not entered on the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods. Use is limited to the purposes, sites, and personnel covered by the approved ethics application TO CONFIRM: HREC reference. Using the apps or materials outside that scope is not authorised.

02 Not a diagnosis or clinical advice

Scores, summaries, charts, and reports produced by these apps are aids to assessment. They are not a diagnosis, and they are not a clinical recommendation.

  • Output must be interpreted by a suitably qualified clinician, within their scope of practice, together with clinical judgement, history, and other assessment information.
  • Nothing in these apps or materials creates a clinician–patient relationship, and nothing replaces professional medical, developmental, or allied health advice.
  • Decisions about a person’s care remain the responsibility of the treating clinician and their health service.

Not for emergencies. These apps are not monitored for urgent clinical issues. If someone needs urgent care, contact your local emergency service (000 in Australia) or the treating health service directly.

We take reasonable care to keep the apps and published materials accurate and current, but they are provided “as is” and “as available”, without warranties of any kind beyond those that cannot be excluded by law.

  • No availability or uptime commitment is given. Planned maintenance, outages, and third-party service interruptions can occur.
  • Users and their organisations remain responsible for any clinical or research records they are required to keep under their own policies and under the approved protocol.
