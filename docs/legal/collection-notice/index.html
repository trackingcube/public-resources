01 Notice — copy for the activation page

TO CONFIRM: institution name collects your name, email address, organisation, and role to create a YourTrack account and give you access to the apps an administrator approved for you (for example Tracking Cube, iRNDA / LMS, and the Portal).

There is no public sign-up. Your group requested access and an administrator approved it. We use Auth0 to sign you in (your password is stored by Auth0, not in plaintext) and Amazon Web Services in Sydney to host the apps. Auth0 may process identity information outside Australia TO CONFIRM: Auth0 tenant region.

If you do not complete activation we cannot create a working login. We also keep login and security logs to operate and protect the service.

This notice is not the participant information sheet. Research data about participants is handled under the approved ethics application and PICF.

You can ask to access or correct your account details, or make a privacy complaint, through the institution privacy office: TO CONFIRM: email. See the institution Privacy Policy TO CONFIRM: URL and the YourTrack platform addendum. You may also contact the OAIC: https://www.oaic.gov.au.
